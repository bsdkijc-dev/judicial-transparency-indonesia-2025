## 3. Key Reconciliation Exceptions Identified

1. **December 2025 Bank vs. Closing Balance Variance (-Rp 176,049,400,875.55):** Driven primarily by *Biaya Konsinyasi* in Ditjen Badilum where December Bank Balance (`Rp 5,130,676,049,963.35`) exceeds Reported Closing Balance (`Rp 4,955,040,511,656.36`), plus *Biaya Perkara* (`-Rp 342.71M`), offset by *Biaya Eksekusi* (`+Rp 5.05M`), *Titipan Perkara Pidana* (`+Rp 10.00M`), and *PHI* (`+Rp 2.82M`).

2. **Buku Keuangan Niaga & Lainnya (Dec 2025):** Tabel 141 reports `Rp 7,935,481,054.00` (*Niaga*, Badilum) and `Rp 6,165,325,720.00` (*Lainnya*, Badilag) in Bank/Receipts columns without corresponding Closing Balance lines.
