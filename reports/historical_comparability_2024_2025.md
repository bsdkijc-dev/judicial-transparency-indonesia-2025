# Historical Comparability Assessment — FY 2024 vs FY 2025

- **Accounting Basis:** Both FY 2024 and FY 2025 are presented under **PP No. 71 Tahun 2010** (Accrual SAP) via **SAKTI** — methodologically comparable at the Face Statement level.
- **Key Structural & Policy Shifts Affecting Line-Item Comparability:**
  1. **Judge Salary Reclassification & Increases:** PP No. 41/2025 & PP No. 42/2025 plus 1,451 new judges shifted major personnel costs from `5111` (PNS, -45.56% to Rp 2.67T) to `5113` (Pejabat Negara, +4,537.76% to Rp 3.29T).
  2. **Budget Efficiency (Inpres No. 1/2025):** Automatic Adjustment (AA) block of **Rp 646,458,381,000** reduced Domestic Travel (`5241`) by -37.74% and Foreign Travel (`5242`) by -59.13%.
  3. **PNBP Account Migration:** Housing rent shifted from `425131` (-54.93%) to `425151` (*Pendapatan Penggunaan Sarana dan Prasarana sesuai Tusi*, Rp 7.58B in 2025 vs Rp 0 in 2024).
